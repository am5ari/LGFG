About Us

Confidence. Dignity. Opportunity.

Helping job seekers prepare for successful reentry into the workforce and community.

OUR MISSION

Helping people prepare for their next chapter.

Look Good Feel Good Lexington is dedicated to empowering individuals in recovery and reentry by providing essential resources that help people regain confidence and prepare for reentry into the workforce.

01

Professional Attire

Quality professional clothing and accessories to help individuals make a strong first impression during interviews and in the workplace.

02

Haircuts & Grooming

Free haircuts and grooming services designed to help job seekers present a polished appearance and build confidence.

03

Support Services

Connections to recovery assistance, housing services, job placement, and other community resources.
