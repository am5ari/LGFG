ABOUT US

More than a service. A community of support.

Look Good, Feel Good Lex empowers individuals re-entering the workforce by providing clothing, haircuts, and essential resources.

We’re committed to boosting confidence and creating opportunities for success in our community.

Our work brings practical support and community connections together to help individuals feel prepared for their next opportunity.
