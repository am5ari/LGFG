ABOUT LOOK GOOD FEEL GOOD LEXINGTON

Empowering people to look good, feel good and move forward.

Look Good Feel Good Lexington is dedicated to empowering individuals in recovery and reentry by connecting them with resources that help build confidence, dignity and opportunity.

OUR MISSION

Helping people feel prepared for their next opportunity.

Look Good Feel Good Lexington is dedicated to empowering individuals in recovery and reentry by providing essential resources, including professional attire suitable for manufacturing and skilled trades jobs, haircuts, and support services.

Our one-day event aims to help job seekers regain confidence and prepare for reentry into the workforce, fostering a sense of dignity, empowerment, and opportunity for a brighter future.

More than clothing. We connect people with opportunity.

Our work brings together practical resources and community support to help job seekers prepare for their next step.

01

Professional Attire

Providing quality professional clothing and accessories to help job seekers make a strong first impression during interviews and in the workplace.

02

Haircuts & Grooming

Offering free haircuts and grooming services to help job seekers present a well-groomed and polished appearance while building confidence.

03

Career Support

Supporting job seekers with resume preparation, interview preparation and connections to local employment opportunities.

04

Community Resources

Connecting individuals with organizations and agencies that can provide additional support such as housing, healthcare, mental health and education.

05

Meals

Providing free meals to participants and creating a welcoming environment where attendees can focus on taking advantage of available resources.

06

Confidence & Opportunity

Creating an environment centered around dignity, empowerment and the opportunity to move forward.

WHERE IT STARTED

Our first event brought the community together.

Our first Look Good Feel Good event was held on March 28, 2025 at the Davis Park Workforce Center.

The event brought together job seekers, vendors, employers, service organizations and community supporters to provide practical resources and encouragement.
